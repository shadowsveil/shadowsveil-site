ShadowsVeil • Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 17, 2026

ShadowsVeil ("the app", "we", "us") is a wallpaper gallery for Android, published by Colby Adams. This policy describes what information is collected when you use ShadowsVeil, why, and what you can do about it.

We tried to keep this short and honest. If something here is unclear, email colby.quentin.adams@gmail.com.

1. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or contacts.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not read, record, or upload your photos or files.
  • We do not sell personal information to anyone, ever.

2. What is collected, and by whom

Wallpaper content (Firebase)

Wallpaper images, thumbnails, titles, keywords, and metadata are stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage). When the app opens, it requests these public records from Firebase so the gallery can populate. Google may log standard request metadata (IP address, user agent, timestamp) as part of operating that service. We do not attach your identity to those requests.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

ShadowsVeil is ad-supported. We show banner and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. AdMob uses a device-level advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. Depending on your region and consent choices, AdMob may collect:

  • A Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Coarse approximate location derived from IP
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Aggregate ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Advertising Policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

Crash and performance diagnostics (optional)

If crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics) is enabled in a future release, anonymized crash traces and device context may be collected to fix bugs. This is disclosed in the Play Store Data Safety section if/when enabled.

3. Your choices

Reset or disable advertising ID

On Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) to reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising.

Opt out of ads entirely (EEA / UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the first time you open ShadowsVeil you'll see a Google-provided consent form asking whether ads can use your data for personalization. You can change that answer any time from the app's settings screen (if present) or by clearing app data.

Children

ShadowsVeil is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from users under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region).

4. Permissions the app requests

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to download wallpapers from Firebase.
  • SET_WALLPAPER — to apply the selected image as your home, lock, or both screens when you tap Apply.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — required by the wallpaper-apply library on older Android versions to stage the downloaded image before handing it to the system wallpaper service. The app does not read any of your personal files.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because ShadowsVeil does not collect personal data tied to your identity, there is no user account to delete. Ad-related data is retained by Google according to the Google AdMob retention policies linked above. To request deletion of ad-related data associated with your device, you can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

If for any reason you would like us to investigate or remove data associated with your device, email colby.quentin.adams@gmail.com with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Third parties

  • Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Storage) — content delivery.
  • Google AdMob — advertising.
  • Google Play — app distribution and install analytics.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the revised version will be published at the same URL. Material changes will be noted in the next app release.
